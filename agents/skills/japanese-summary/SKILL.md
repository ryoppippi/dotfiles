Summarise this session concisely in Japanese, as bullet points, under these
headings; omit a heading with nothing to report:

1. **目的** — what the user set out to do
2. **実施内容** — what was done and changed
3. **結果** — the outcome, and whether the goal was met
4. **未完了事項** — remaining tasks or open issues
5. **備考** — decisions, trade-offs, or context worth remembering

Stick to facts and outcomes rather than process.
